#what is lidar

Robots camera is not that great for estimations of distances. Cameras 
rely on extra light source
Computers need a sensor to estimate distances and images.

A lidar measures with photons and emits some light.

The time to travel there and back is how it measures the distances.
A camera is gives photographs but lidar measures directly how far away it is.

passive versus active sensor.
Lidar is an active sensor- laser emitted and bounces back. 
It tells you how far away objects are
